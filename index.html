Data Engineering • Process Automation Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK)

OJK Regulatory Data Matching Automation Pipeline

High-throughput data cleaning and fuzzy-matching engine integrating disparate insurance supervisory data streams with zero manual cross-checking.

01. Problem

Heterogeneous databases (SIPO & SiPEDULI) had naming inconsistencies and typos, necessitating hundreds of hours of manual verification.

02. Analysis

Analyzed 60,000+ financial rows, identifying systematic punctuation errors, truncated corporate prefixes (PT, CV), and phonetic variations.

03. Approach

Constructed Python pipelines using Pandas and RapidFuzz (token sort ratio & partial ratio algorithms) with automated confidence grading.

04. Outcome

95%+ match accuracy, 40% reduction in processing cycle, and 20+ automated statutory reports ready for supervisory audit.
